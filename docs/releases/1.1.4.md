# Sideby 1.1.4

**A smaller, softer circular menu that leaves more room for your work.**

- The default circular menu is now **384 points across**, down from 440. The footer is also shorter, while additional displays still get more room for their previews.
- Rounded sector corners, thinner outlines, subtle shading, and lighter text make the menu feel less heavy. Hover and keyboard focus still clearly identify the action you are about to take.
- Connection names retain a minimum readable size and can use two lines. Display names and Space numbers remain separate in the center preview, and the footer shows the hovered connection's full name.
- Connection management and Close now share one compact bar. Empty-state guidance stays clear of the center save action, including at the smallest supported size.
- Pointer and drag targets follow the rendered sectors, including their rounded corners. Changing between Circle and Table still lets you release the opening shortcut to close immediately.

Your saved connections, names, ordering, and shortcuts are preserved. This update changes the menu's presentation, without changing how Sideby connects your existing Spaces.

**Update:** choose **Settings → General → Check for Updates**, or open the DMG and drag Sideby to Applications. Requires Apple silicon and macOS 14 or later.
