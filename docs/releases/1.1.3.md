# Sideby 1.1.3

**Open and close the quick switcher with less delay, read connections more easily, and get clearer help when a Space needs attention.**

- Releasing the opening shortcut now closes the quick switcher even after changing between **Circle** and **Table**. Starting an edit, saving, or reordering still keeps the window open so you can finish the action.
- Shortcut-held circular menus appear without the entrance spring and close immediately on release. Pressing and releasing the shortcut also avoids unnecessary view updates.
- The circular menu has clearer keyboard focus outlines, larger minimum text sizes, two-line connection names, and separate display names and Space numbers. Focused edit controls remain visible and clickable.
- The quick table labels its connection action **Go together** and uses **Check Spaces** when a connection needs review. Connection management is available in the title row.
- Optional first-connection guidance highlights where to connect another display and explains how to reuse the connection you have already saved.
- Recovery messages distinguish unavailable displays, unreadable state, and Spaces that need review. They name the affected connection and lead to refresh or a repair bar in the editor, where you can choose a replacement explicitly.

Your saved connections, names, ordering, and shortcuts are preserved. Sideby still connects existing Spaces; it does not recreate desktops or restore app windows.

**Update:** choose **Settings → General → Check for Updates**, or open the DMG and drag Sideby to Applications. Requires Apple silicon and macOS 14 or later.
