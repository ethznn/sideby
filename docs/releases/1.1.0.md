# Sideby 1.1.0

**Switch your connected Spaces from a circular menu beside your pointer.**

- Hold **⌥⇧Space** to open the new circular switcher. Click a connection to move its Spaces together. The menu stays within the screen, with transparent gaps, clear hover feedback, and smooth opening and closing animations that respect Reduce Motion.
- Click **Save current Spaces** in the center to add what you are looking at. Sideby fills in the name from the Space titles and avoids duplicate connections. With no connections yet, **Create first connection** and a current-Space preview show where to start.
- Hover over a connection to reveal its **pencil** and **trash** buttons. Clicking the name switches Spaces; only the pencil renames. Removing one connection is immediate, without a confirmation dialog. Your actual Spaces and app windows stay open.
- **Undo** remains visible, including after deleting the last connection. Restore up to 20 connection changes, even after reopening Sideby. **Clear all…** is available in the switcher and asks for confirmation before clearing everything.
- Use **Edit connections** for the Space list and drag editor, or **Table** for the detailed switching view. Existing connections, manual order, names, and numbered shortcuts are preserved. More than eight connections are split into pages.
- Settings now shows each display’s Space count, a control to reveal more Spaces, and clearer differences between source cards, connected cells, and empty drop targets. English labels use **Space 1**, **Space 2**, and so on for list order.
- First use and empty legacy migrations no longer create a placeholder connection with nothing assigned. Existing saved connections are kept. Name editing also focuses its text field reliably when the editor opens.

Releasing the opening shortcut alone closes the circular menu; it does not save or switch anything. Saving or editing keeps the menu open so you can release the keys. Use Tab to focus controls, Return/Space to activate them, and Escape to close.

The English and Korean README and website include updated product screenshots, a 28-second usage tutorial covering the circular menu and deletion recovery, and a refreshed 22.5-second promotional film.

**Update:** choose **Settings → General → Check for Updates**, or open the DMG and drag Sideby to Applications. Requires Apple silicon and macOS 14 or later.
