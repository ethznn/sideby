# Sideby 1.1.1

**Preview each connection more clearly and switch with less visual interruption.**

- Hover over a circular-menu sector to see its connected Spaces in the center. Hover over the center to reveal **Add current setup**. The current-connection marker and hover highlight now use distinct appearances.
- Connection names are larger and easier to read, with more room for long names and a clearer separation from shortcuts and editing buttons.
- Connections with three or more displays show each display's contents in the center. The menu grows with the display count when opened and keeps its size as you hover between connections. If the available screen area is too small, Sideby uses the table view.
- Very short switches no longer briefly flash a connection-name HUD. Longer switches fade the HUD in and out, and consecutive switches reuse the visible HUD instead of recreating it.
- Circular-menu refreshes after a Space switch are grouped while retaining the existing click-recovery behavior.

Your saved connections, names, ordering, and shortcuts are preserved. Sideby still connects existing Spaces; it does not recreate desktops or restore app windows.

**Update:** choose **Settings → General → Check for Updates**, or open the DMG and drag Sideby to Applications. Requires Apple silicon and macOS 14 or later.
