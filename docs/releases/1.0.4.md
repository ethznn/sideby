# Sideby 1.0.4

**Make quick adjustments without losing your place.**

- **Edit directly in the quick matrix:** hold **⌥⇧Space**, then use the pencil to rename a connection. Clicking a connection name, Space cell, or arrow switches its assigned displays. Drag names to reorder columns, or drag cells within the same display row to move or swap. Release the opening shortcut before editing, then hold Option while dropping to copy.
- **Steadier connection editing:** clearing all connections and adding the first Space keeps the heading, instructions, Space list, and drop targets in place. Results and Undo share the existing status area, without scrolling the editor away from your work.
- **Windows that fit their contents:** the quick matrix measures its content when opened and keeps long content scrollable within the display. Space-selection lists are constrained to the screen, with clearer button-shaped choices.
- **Recover missing Spaces:** a missing assignment shows an explicit repair action. Choose another Space, disconnect the cell, or Option-drag a Space from the same display row to copy it. Undo restores the previous assignment.
- **Clearer pointer feedback:** connection columns and pencil buttons have distinct hover states, and clickable controls use the pointing-hand cursor.

The English and Korean README and website include refreshed product screenshots and a new **25-second usage GIF**: drag to connect two displays, connect current Spaces, switch from coding to PR review, swap, undo, Option-copy, rename, and return with **⌥⇧Tab**. The tutorial uses actual product views with fictional data and reconstructed pointer motion and transitions.

Existing connections, custom names, shortcuts, and undo history are preserved. Sideby connects existing Spaces; it does not recreate desktops, launch apps, or restore window positions.

**Update:** choose **Settings → General → Check for Updates**, or open the DMG and drag Sideby to Applications. Requires Apple silicon and macOS 14 or later.
