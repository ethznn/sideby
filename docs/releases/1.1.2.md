# Sideby 1.1.2

**Choose the view that suits your work, reorder connections directly, and switch views with less delay.**

- Switch between **Circle** and **Table** using the control above the quick switcher. Its position stays fixed while the window fits each view, and Sideby remembers your choice for next time.
- Drag a connection in the circular menu to change its order without switching Spaces. **Undo** restores the previous order; Space assignments and numbered shortcuts are preserved.
- Switching views reuses existing content and avoids repeated connection calculations. Large tables create columns as needed, and app/window titles refresh in the background without blocking view changes.
- The entire Circle or Table button is now clickable, including the empty area around its label. The click target stays the same whether the view is selected or not.
- **Edit connections** hides the view control and its empty space. **Back to switching** returns to your previous view and restores the control in the same position.
- Returning to either view reveals the current connection. Background title results are discarded if the underlying Spaces have changed, and Space pickers read the latest available choices.

Your saved connections, names, ordering, and shortcuts are preserved. Sideby still connects existing Spaces; it does not recreate desktops or restore app windows.

**Update:** choose **Settings → General → Check for Updates**, or open the DMG and drag Sideby to Applications. Requires Apple silicon and macOS 14 or later.
