# Sideby 1.0.2

**Connect just the Spaces you need, clean up several connections together, and undo changes with confidence.**

- **Select several connections:** choose **Select connections** at the table's top left, then click headings or cells to select their columns. Select all is available too. Confirm once to remove the selected columns; one **Undo** restores the whole batch. Selecting columns never edits their cells or switches Spaces.
- **Clear only what you need:** disconnect one cell, clear a display's connections from its **…** menu, or clear all connections. Connections on disconnected displays can also be cleaned up. Other connections and their numbered shortcuts are kept.
- **Start again immediately:** an empty table still offers cells where you can choose or drop a Space. Create your first connection directly, or use **Connect in current order** to pair all Spaces by position.
- **Undo up to 20 changes:** connection edit history survives restarting Sideby. You can clear connections, rebuild them, and step back through those changes without a separate save or backup screen.
- **Keep the list current:** visible connection editors automatically refresh available Space and app/window titles. **Read again** appears when Spaces cannot be read.
- **Use your app language for updates:** update dialogs follow Sideby's language preference after reopening the app. Settings tells you when a restart is needed. Release notes are provided in English.

English and Korean onboarding, README, and website instructions have been updated, including the removal of outdated pre-1.0 guidance.

Existing connections, names, and shortcuts are preserved. Editing or removing connections does not move or delete actual Spaces or close app windows. Sideby connects existing Spaces; it does not create Spaces, launch apps, or restore window layouts.

**Update:** choose **Settings → General → Check for Updates**, or open the DMG and drag Sideby to Applications. Requires Apple silicon and macOS 14 or later.
